Real-time AI intelligence for token launches

GRAID is an autonomous on-chain agent that scores every token launch before the outcome exists — then returns to verify whether it graduated.

what this is

GRAID is an autonomous analyst that does not sleep. It watches every launch on Robinhood Chain, reads it straight off the chain the second it lands, and scores it with a model initially fitted on 600 launches and calibrated against thousands of live outcomes. The guess is written down before the answer exists.

Two hours later the agent goes back, checks what actually happened, and updates its own hit rate in public — misses included. Nobody edits the record and nobody picks the examples. It does not trade, never touches a wallet, and does not predict price: only whether a launch gains real traction.
