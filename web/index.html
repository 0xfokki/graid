what this is

GRAID is an autonomous analyst that does not sleep. It watches every launch on Robinhood Chain, reads it straight off the chain the second it lands, and scores it with a model calibrated against thousands of live outcomes. More than 15,000 launches have been judged this way. The guess is written down before the answer exists.

Later the agent goes back, checks what actually happened, and updates its own hit rate in public — misses included. Nobody edits the record and nobody picks the examples. It does not trade, never touches a wallet, and does not predict price: only whether a launch gains real traction.

how it scores

what we said against what happened
p = sigmoid( a · logit( Σ wi ) + b )
seven featureseach one read off the chain at the moment the launch appears
empirical rateshow often the event followed in that bucket, shrunk towards the base rate
log-oddsthe buckets are summed as evidence, not multiplied as probabilities
calibrationa and b are fitted on resolved outcomes and validated on a later slice
no language modelnothing in the scoring path generates text

Traction means outside money — buys that are not part of the launch transaction, not from the deployer, and not from a wallet the deployer declared tax-exempt. It counts when those buys reach a tenth of the migration threshold inside a fixed window measured from launch. Not a prediction that the price goes up.
